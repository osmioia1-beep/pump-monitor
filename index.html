How metrics are calculated

Completed cycles are assigned to the day they end. Running time is split across days and includes the open cycle up to the snapshot. Average, maximum and minimum durations use completed cycles only. Daily rates use the full calendar window; incomplete days show N/A. Global cycles/day includes every observed calendar date, including idle and partial dates.
